## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Creative narrative contracts are maintained
The maintained backend workflows SHALL execute deterministic emotional arc, foreshadowing, novelty, and preference contracts.

#### Scenario: Narrative quality regression
- **WHEN** a creative narrative invariant regresses
- **THEN** both maintained workflows fail before release.

### Requirement: Creative workflow parity is preserved
The maintained coverage and backend-test workflow lists SHALL include the promoted suites in identical order.

#### Scenario: Ordered parity
- **WHEN** test paths are extracted from both workflows
- **THEN** the ordered lists are identical.
